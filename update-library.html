Step 1 · Choose your spreadsheet

Update Track Library

Your spreadsheet is the master copy of your library. Choose it here (.xlsx or CSV with the columns SONG, ARTIST, DATE, DECADE, CATEGORY and optionally BYPASS), check the data quality report, then create Tracks.json for your website.

Step 3 · Put it on your website

Tracks.json created ✓

  1. Open your Downloads folder and find Tracks.json. If your browser named it something like Tracks (1).json, rename it to exactly Tracks.json — capital T.
  2. In your GitHub repo, go to the folder with index.html and choose Add file → Upload files.
  3. Drop Tracks.json in and press Commit changes. It replaces the old one.
  4. Wait a minute or two, then open Search By Track Name — it shows the new track count and date. If it still shows the old list, refresh after a few more minutes.

Step 2 · Data quality report

Here's what was found

Tick or untick fixes below; the numbers update as you go. Nothing changes until you press Create Tracks.json.

Bypassed rows (BYPASS = Y)

These rows skip every fix and check below. They go into Tracks.json exactly as typed in your spreadsheet — no capitals tidying, spelling fixes, artist spellings, year or category checks, and they're never left out as duplicates. Only spaces at the very start or end are removed. To check a row again, change its BYPASS to N.

Spelling & grammar

Ticked fixes go into Tracks.json and the corrected spreadsheet — your original file isn't changed. Sure fixes are pre-ticked; guesses are left for you to decide.

Artists spelt more than one way

Pick the spelling to use for every track by that artist. Differences in capitals or punctuation are pre-picked; possible typos are left as typed until you choose — the most common spelling in your sheet isn't always the right one.

Duplicates

Exact duplicates are always left out. Close matches (different punctuation, "The", "&" / "and") are left out too unless you tick Keep anyway.

Problems — left out of Tracks.json

Fix these rows in your spreadsheet and choose it again. The corrected spreadsheet keeps them, with the problem written in a NOTES column.
